Who this course is for

This MHA elective is for people who want to understand the AI tools they encounter and decide when to use them. Bring a question about a healthcare product or a task you’d like to make easier in your own life.

Questions you might bring and skills you’ll practice

What you’ll practice
When I give an AI tool a document, am I training it?
Use a sample policy to explain the difference between training a model and giving it something to look up when it answers.
How can I tell if an AI summary missed something important?
Check a draft checklist against the memo it came from. Mark what the tool got right, what it left out, and what someone should verify before using it.
A vendor says its software saves time. Whose time, and compared with what?
Read a vendor’s claim about faster insurance reviews. Identify what it measured and what you’d still want to know about errors, appeals, or delays for patients.
Which parts of a healthcare task should a person handle?
Follow a fictional medication request. Decide what software can prepare, who checks it, and what should happen if a required detail is missing.
Could I build a tool for something I do by hand every week?
Pick a task from home, school, or work. Build a small tool with AI, test it with three inputs, and watch someone try it using your instructions.

The opening survey asks what you already use and what you’d like to figure out. Your questions help shape the first class.
