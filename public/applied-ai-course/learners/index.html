WHO IT’S FOR & WHY IT MATTERS

Get more comfortable using and building with AI.

For MHA students at any career stage. You might be new to AI, use it every day, or still be figuring out where it helps.

WHY THIS CLASS

Learn how it works and try it for yourself.

You might use AI for recipes, meeting notes, or a first draft. That can leave plenty of questions about how it works and when to trust it.

In this class, we’ll test tools, discuss how healthcare companies use AI, and build our own projects. You’ll get practice asking questions and checking what works.

Bring the experience you have.

Healthcare experience and AI experience are different things. Wherever you’re starting, you’ll choose a project that interests you.

BRING YOUR QUESTIONS

“I’ve been wondering…”

Explore the questions we’ll work through.

UnderstandWhat do “model,” “LLM,” and “agent” actually mean?

Learn what the terms mean, who makes the tools, and how an AI system produces an answer.

ChooseWhich tool fits what I’m trying to do?

Compare tools for the same task, from everyday assistants to healthcare software and agents. Decide which one is worth trying and why.

CheckHow do I know whether an answer is any good?

Check answers against their sources, notice missing details, and decide what information you can share with a tool.

Understand the limits →
EvaluateA vendor says it uses AI. What should I ask next?

Discuss examples from care delivery, insurance, and pharmacy. Look at what the company promised, what happened, and what the evidence leaves unanswered.

BuildCould I make a tool of my own?

Use AI-assisted coding to turn a small idea into something another person can try. Learn to describe what you want, test the result, and improve it.

ApplyHow could this help in my own life or work?

Choose something you’d like to make easier at home, at school, or at work. Build a small tool and test whether it helps.
