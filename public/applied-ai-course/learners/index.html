Who this course is for

This MHA elective is for people who want to know where AI can help and how to use it well. You might be getting started, already using ChatGPT, or evaluating tools for your workplace.

You might be thinking about these questions and skills

What you’ll practice
I know I should be using AI, but I’m not sure where to start.

Choose an everyday task, give an AI tool clear instructions and appropriate information, and check the result. Leave with one useful way to start using it.

10 weeks · Week 15 weeks · Week 1

I use ChatGPT, but what else could I be doing with AI?

Explore document questions, meeting notes, spreadsheets, and AI features inside work software. Match a task to a suitable tool and identify something useful to try beyond your current habits.

10 weeks · Week 45 weeks · Week 1

I need to evaluate AI vendors at work. What should I ask?

Use a scorecard to examine a healthcare vendor’s evidence, data access, workflow fit, and costs. Decide what a small trial would need to show before you recommend the product.

10 weeks · Week 45 weeks · Week 2

Which AI concepts and principles should I understand?

Explain the terms you hear, including models, training, fine-tuning, prompts, and document retrieval. Use those ideas to judge what information a tool needs, check its answers, and decide where a person should stay involved.

10 weeks · Week 25 weeks · Week 1

Could I build something for my personal life or automate part of my work?

Describe a task to an AI coding tool, try what it makes, and improve it. Build something another person can use, test ordinary and unexpected inputs, and explain which steps still need human review.

10 weeks · Week 55 weeks · Week 2

The opening survey asks what you already use and what you’d like to figure out. Your questions help shape the first class.
