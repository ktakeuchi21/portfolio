INSTRUCTOR · UNIVERSITY OF UTAH MHA ALUMNUS

Kai Takeuchi

I work in healthcare product and use AI every day.

I build tools with AI for work and personal life. I also evaluate companies selling AI products, asking what the technology does and whether it solves a problem worth solving.

View my professional background ↗

WHY I WANT TO TEACH THIS

Help students feel ready to use AI.

As an MHA alumnus, I’d like our program to give students more practice with AI. I want them to understand the terms they hear, know what tools can and can’t do, and feel comfortable trying things themselves.

I’d bring questions from product work and show how I use AI to build tools, including the mistakes along the way. Students would question vendor claims and make something they care about, whatever their background.
