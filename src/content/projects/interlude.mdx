## The product question

A spare five minutes is easy to spend choosing what to learn. Articles, videos, and podcasts offer more material than a small moment can hold. Interlude starts with a narrower question: how can a learning app offer one useful next step and connect those moments over time?

This is a private product exploration, with my role spanning product direction and prototyping. The intended experience moves between an iPhone for everyday listening and a laptop for reading, notes, and course management. A clear stopping point matters as much as an easy start.

## The experience

A learner can resume a short lesson, read or listen, inspect its sources, and try an optional recall question or practical reflection. Courses provide continuity; saved topics leave room to explore. A selected listening queue supports a longer session without turning the library into an endless feed.

Positions, notes, and learning history belong to the lesson version actually used. Revised teaching can become current without erasing an earlier completion or moving a note onto different wording. Publisher video and podcast embeds supplement the original lessons; they remain separate from the saved narration and learning record.

The app separates research, lesson preparation, publication, and audio generation. Each operation can preserve completed output when another step fails. Current narration uses Google Chirp 3 HD; previously saved OpenAI audio remains available. Runtime questions and other AI-assisted features have their own bounded provider paths.

## Decisions and design lessons

**Content changes should preserve the learner’s history.** A new lesson version can improve the teaching without silently changing what someone previously completed. Notes, practice, and narration need that same version boundary.

**Listening and generation are different experiences.** Saved audio makes returning to a lesson predictable. An interrupted provider request should be recoverable without automatically generating and charging for the same material again.

**Activity is not proof of understanding.** Reading, completing a supplement, answering a recall question, and applying an idea are different signals. Keeping them separate gives progress a more useful meaning than an undifferentiated completion count.

## Current state and next step

Interlude has a private deployed version with saved narration, and the current source contains three foundation courses. Implementation records document hosted playback checks and provider integration. The broader experience is still evolving; those checks do not establish improved learning outcomes.

I currently prepare and publish new content through a separate research workflow. The app handles runtime AI requests independently of that process. The next evaluation is whether learners can resume after interruptions and retain a concept later, beyond simply finishing a lesson.
